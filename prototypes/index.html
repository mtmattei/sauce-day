cut the bad

Three directions for the same thirteen-tab workbook. Same real data in all three — 2020 through 2025 from the sheets, the 2026 plan as it stands — so what changes between them is only the design.

All three share one drawn layer: a tomato icon family rooted in the cut mark, grappa bottles built from geometry and scaled to price, and photobook plates that hold their shape before there is anything in them. Nothing here waits on an asset.

A

Slab paper ledger

One continuous sheet of warm paper. No cards, no shadows, hairlines only. The navigation is a thirteen-notch index down the left edge; every notch is held back from the spine rule and the one you are on runs out to meet it. Anything bought gets cut with a tomato line. Photobook is a printed contact sheet, captions in serif italic.

Instrument Serif + Newsreader · warm off-white · engraved hairline icons · Fukasawa-dominant

 B

Counter instrument

A measuring device rather than a website. One typeface across nine weights, zero radii, a strict grid. The tabs are thirteen graduations on a rule, and a readout rail down the left holds the six live figures on every single tab. Grappa is a rig: bottles measured against a graduated price axis with the record drawn as a datum. Photobook is a numbered archive index.

Chivo · warm graphite, light mode included · geometric icons · Puckett-dominant, highest density

 C

Line editorial

No chrome at all. A single hairline down the left edge is the entire navigation — it stays quiet until you approach it, then the thirteen names and their marks bloom out. Big display numbers, a margin column of annotations beside every table. Photobook runs one band a year with the year set huge; grappa is a shelf you read at a glance.

Bricolage Grotesque + Schibsted Grotesk · near-white · the blend

All thirteen tabs work in each. Arrow keys move between them; the URL hash keeps your place and deep links land. Serve the folder over HTTP (python -m http.server 8080) rather than opening the files directly, so data.js and visuals.js load.

To add a real photograph, set a plate's src in data.js under photobook. To add a grappa shot, set photo on that year in grappa. Frames keep their aspect either way, so pictures drop in without moving the page.
